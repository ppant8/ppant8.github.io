Hello! I’m **Pragya Pant**, a GIS analyst and geospatial researcher dedicated to climate-cryosphere data and interactions.

My work focuses on **Insect ecology**, **Integrated Pest Management (IPM)**, and **insect-plant trophic interactions**. I am passionate about insect ecology, understanding insect behavior, interactions, and their roles in agroecosystem and applying scientific insights to improving crop health, and promoting food security through eco-friendly pest and disease management strategies.

Currently, I serve as a **Project Coordinator at SAHAS Nepal**, leading climate-resilient agriculture initiatives. Previously, I worked with iDE Nepal on the USAID IPM project, where I supported the mass production of beneficial insects and implemented field-based biological pest control trials focusing on integrated pest management.

📘 My academic training includes an M.S. in Entomology and a B.Sc. in Agriculture from Agriculture and Forestry University, Nepal. My research has contributed to the scientific understanding of pest behavior, including the management of the pulse beetle using natural oils. I have experience in field-based and lab-based research.

Outside of work, I enjoy **hiking**, **trekking**, **travelling**, and learning from diverse cultures and landscapes.

If you'd like to collaborate or learn more, feel free to reach out via [email](mailto:pragya.pant@mnsu.edu) or connect with me on [LinkedIn](http://www.linkedin.com/in/pragya-pant-889302286).
