This project is an innovative community-based recycling idea with an aim to address the issue of **waste management** and promote **environmental sustainability**. 
*Pragya Pant*, Asmita Rawat

### 💡 Impact

This program trained students to collect and donate recyclable materials such as plastic bottles, cans, papers etc. that can be transformed into artistic and functional, promoting the concept of circular economy where waste is not seen as a valuable resource.

### Objectives:
- Promote recycling through student involvement: By creating functional and artistic objects from recycled materials, the project aims to promote recycling and encourage students to view waste as a valuable resource.
- Raise awareness about the environmental impact of waste through innovative community- based recycling idea.
- To promote creativity and artistic expression while reducing waste through the creation of artistic and functional objects from recycled materials.

### 🌱Activities and Implementation Strategy:
1. Selection of School:
- Research and identifying suitable schools in Baneshwor, Kathmandu for implementation.
- Consideration of factors such as willingness to participate, availability of resources, and community support.
2. Collaboration with School authority:
- Discussion of the project objectives and their collaboration and support.
- Establishment of partnerships to ensure proper waste sorting and processing.
3. Orientation to Students:
- Conducting orientation sessions in the selected school to raise awareness about waste management, recycling, and the project objectives.
- Explanation of the importance of waste reduction and the concept of upcycling.
- Encouraging students to actively participate in the program and motivate them to collect and donate recyclable materials.
4. Collection and Processing of Wastes:
- Encourage students to collect waste or unused items from their own homes.
5. Creation of Artistic Items:
- Conducting workshops and creative sessions to guide students in the process of creating unique items.
- Encourage students by allowing them to express their artistic skills while utilizing recycled materials.
