# Hi! I am Pragya Pant
My journey in geospatial science began with a Bachelor’s degree in Geomatics Engineering from Kathmandu University, Nepal, where I developed a strong foundation in surveying, mapping, and spatial data analysis. Driven by a desire to understand the complex relationships between the Earth's surface, climate, and environmental change, I continued my academic journey at Minnesota State University, Mankato, where I am pursuing a Master’s degree in Geographic Information Science (GISc). Through my academic research, teaching, and practical experience, I have continued to expand my skills in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, and spatial modeling to investigate real-world environmental challenges.

I am particularly passionate about mountain environments, climate change, and the cryosphere, with a research focus on mapping potential permafrost zones across the Himalaya using remote sensing and machine learning. I am interested in understanding how changing environmental conditions affect high-mountain landscapes, ecosystem stability, and communities that depend on these environments. By integrating geospatial technologies, environmental data, and emerging analytical methods, I aim to contribute to a better understanding of climate-driven changes and support evidence-based environmental monitoring and decision-making.

This portfolio showcases my academic projects, geospatial analyses, research progress, and technical skills as I continue to grow as a geospatial researcher. I welcome opportunities to collaborate, exchange ideas, and connect with researchers and professionals working in GIS, remote sensing, climate science, and mountain environments. Explore my projects, share your thoughts, and connect with me as I work toward advancing geospatial approaches to understanding our changing planet.
