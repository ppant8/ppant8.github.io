My work focuses on Geographic Information Science, remote sensing, and spatial analysis to understand environmental processes and climate change impacts, particularly in high-mountain regions. With a background in Geomatics Engineering and current graduate studies in Geographic Information Science, I apply geospatial technologies, satellite-derived data, and statistical modeling to investigate mountain permafrost, rock glacier distribution, and environmental change in the Himalaya. I am particularly interested in integrating GIS, Earth observation, and machine learning to map potential permafrost zones and improve our understanding of the changing mountain cryosphere and its implications for natural hazards, water resources, and environmental resilience.
