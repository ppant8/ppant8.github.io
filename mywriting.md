My writing explores the intersections of geography, climate change, remote sensing, and environmental science, with an emphasis on communicating complex scientific ideas clearly and effectively. Through research proposals, conference abstracts, technical documentation, academic presentations, and geospatial storytelling, I aim to connect scientific evidence with real-world environmental challenges. I am particularly interested in communicating research on mountain permafrost, climate change, and the cryosphere through accessible writing, maps, visualizations, and interactive GIS applications that support scientific understanding and informed decision-making.
